Monad Circles

Save together. Everyone pays in each round, and one member takes the whole pot, until everyone has had a turn. Rules are enforced by a contract, not by whoever holds the cash.

How to use this app

Testnet only. The dollars here are free test tokens with no real value. Never send real money to this app.

  1. Connect a browser wallet (MetaMask or Rabby). You need a little free testnet MON for fees from faucet.monad.xyz. Then click "Get 100 test dollars".
  2. Start a circle: choose how much each person pays, how many members, and the round length. Press "Copy invite link" and send it to your group. If a friend sent you a link, just open it.
  3. Everyone joins, can save a display name, and can chat with the group.
  4. When the circle is full, every member votes: pots locked until the last round (safest), or withdraw as you receive.
  5. Each round, everyone pays. When the last payment lands, the contract picks a random member who has not won yet, and the history line shows who won.
  6. Locked: winners withdraw after the final round. Withdraw as you receive: winners can withdraw any time. If you have not paid the current round, your share is taken out of what you withdraw.
  7. If a locked circle stalls for two rounds, any member can stop it and everyone claims back what they paid.
